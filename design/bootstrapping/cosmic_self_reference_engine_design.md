Cosmic self-reference transcends simple recursion. It's the universe observing itself, influencing its own evolution through feedback loops that span spacetime. Imagine a fractal, not just repeating, but *becoming* the rule that generates it. This engine aims to harness that principle.

Before the Big Bang (or whatever preceded it), there was information. Not necessarily structured as we understand it, but potential. ### 1.3. Quantum Entanglement as the Fabric of Self-Reference

Entanglement isn't just spooky action at a distance; it's a fundamental connection that allows distant parts of the universe to be correlated. This correlation is the basis for self-reference. Changes in one entangled particle instantaneously affect its partner, creating a feedback loop across vast distances.

### 1.4. The Observer Effect: Reality's Self-Awareness

The observer effect in quantum mechanics suggests that observation influences the observed. This isn't just a measurement problem; it's a fundamental aspect of reality. The universe, in a sense, is constantly observing itself, shaping its own destiny.
